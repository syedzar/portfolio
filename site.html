About

        
Hey! I'm Zaryan, a Computer Engineering Co-op student at the University of Guelph.

        
I'm interested in the intersection of hardware and software, particularly FPGA design, embedded systems, computer architecture, and low-level programming. I enjoy taking what I learn in class and turning it into hands-on projects, whether that's designing a CPU in VHDL, building systems software in C, or working with physical hardware.

        
I'm always looking for opportunities to learn something new, improve my skills, and work on challenging engineering problems. Outside of coursework, I enjoy experimenting with new technologies and building projects that help me better understand how systems work from the hardware level up.
